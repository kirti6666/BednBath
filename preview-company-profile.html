Company Overview

We are a specialized manufacturer and exporter of premium hotel linen, with continuous production since 2010. Our core focus is high-quality bed linen, bath linen and banquet linen designed specifically for the hospitality industry.

We supply 4-star and 5-star hotels and also sell through major international retail platforms such as Amazon and eBay. A significant portion of our production is exported worldwide.

Our Expertise & Experience

  • Continuous manufacturing of hotel-grade linen since 2010
  • Strong specialization in the hotel industry
  • Proven track record of supplying leading 4-star and 5-star hotels
  • Established presence in both B2B (hotels) and B2C (Amazon and eBay) channels
  • Reliable exporter with consistent international shipments

Product Range

  • Bed Linen: Bedsheets, Duvet Covers, Pillowcases, Mattress Protectors, Bed Skirts. High thread count (TC) cotton, poly cotton and luxury blends.
  • Bath Linen: Bath Towels, Hand Towels, Face Towels, Bath Mats, Bathrobes. Soft, highly absorbent and durable hotel-quality towels.
  • Banquet Linen: Table Cloths, Napkins, Runners, Chair Covers, Skirting. Stain-resistant, elegant fabrics for banquets and events.

All products are manufactured to meet the durability, softness and frequent-wash requirements of professional hotel use. View our products.

Key Strengths for International Buyers

  • Long-term production continuity since 2010
  • Experience serving demanding 4-star and 5-star hotel standards
  • Flexible production capacity for bulk orders as well as retail volumes
  • Export-ready packaging and documentation
  • Competitive pricing with consistent quality
  • Customization of sizes, colours, embroidery, logo and packaging to buyer requirements

Markets We Serve

  • 4-star and 5-star hotels (direct supply)
  • International retail platforms (Amazon and eBay, exported products)
  • Hospitality distributors and importers worldwide

Quality Commitment

We maintain strict quality control at every stage of production to ensure our linen meets international hotel standards for strength, colour fastness, softness and longevity after repeated industrial washing.

Work With Us

We welcome inquiries from international buyers, hotel chains, importers and distributors. Contact us for product catalogues, samples, MOQ details and competitive quotations.
